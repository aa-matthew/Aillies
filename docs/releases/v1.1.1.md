# Aillies 1.1.1 — UI and onboarding update

A clearer way to use accounts from your paired computers and share accounts from this computer.

- Separate Aillies Use and Share, with editable device names and independent Codex/Claude controls.
- Guided onboarding, per-service pairing permissions, connection readiness and recent warnings.
- Reviewed quit/switch/reopen on Mac, with desktop apps reopening; terminals and editors stay closed. Manual switching on Windows/Linux.
- Full pairing codes connect across networks through Iroh; eight-digit codes stay nearby-only.
- Safe per-chat Codex migration preserves damaged or unsupported histories and reports content-free diagnostics.
- Missing Claude generation effort defaults to medium on reviewed supported models. Explicit effort and signed history are preserved.
- Disconnect & quit preserves unrelated configuration edits and provides an exit when cleanup needs attention.

Claude remains experimental. Live account entitlement, native image generation and physical cross-network behavior require device-specific acceptance. The Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; Windows installers are unsigned. Assets include Mac Apple Silicon, Windows x64 and Linux desktop packages, CLI archives and source with SHA256 checksums. Previous releases remain available.
